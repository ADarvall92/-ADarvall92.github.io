About Anthony

Stage-trained. Screen-focused.

Anthony Darvall is a British-Australian actor working across feature film, serial drama, theatre and voice.

Conservatory-trained at ACTT, he works across intelligent antagonists, romantic antiheroes, eccentric leads and grounded dramatic roles. His theatre background, physical training and dry humour support screen work that can move between polish, danger, restraint and comedy.

Unrestricted work rights in Australia and the United Kingdom. Skills include fencing, boxing, Brazilian jiu-jitsu, horse riding, archery and extensive accent work.
