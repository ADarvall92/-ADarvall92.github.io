About

Stage-trained.
Screen-focused.

Anthony Darvall is a British-Australian, conservatory-trained actor working across film, theatre and international screen drama.

His work tends toward intelligent antagonists, romantic antiheroes, eccentric leads and characters whose charm may be either a gift or a warning.

His theatre foundation includes Heathcliff in Wuthering Heights, Le Vicomte de Valmont in Les Liaisons Dangereuses, Hamlet, Mercutio and R.P. McMurphy in One Flew Over the Cuckoo’s Nest.

Recent screen work includes the feature lead in Algorithm, alongside feature, short-film and international vertical-drama roles. That work has developed experience in lead and major-character responsibility, fast production environments and audience-facing genre storytelling.

Anthony reads widely and trains across boxing, Brazilian jiu-jitsu, calisthenics and fencing. He brings preparation, curiosity and a dry sense of humour to the room.
